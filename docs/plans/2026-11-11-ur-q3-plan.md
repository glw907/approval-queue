## What the pass changes

Today no part of the queue knows what a round is. The fact check drops a `round` key (mechanism 1), `land` takes exactly two arguments (mechanism 12), and the board, the rail, and the digest page group by initiative or by repo alone.

After this pass a fact file may name its round, and the check refuses a bad round id. A queued packet's `item.json` holds the round. `approval-queue land --round <id>` writes the round into its digest line. The board and the rail gather a round's waiting items under one round heading inside their initiative's group, the digest page groups a repo's lines by round, and a packet's status line names its round.

A round id is "lowercase letters, digits, and hyphens, starting with a letter, 12 characters at most" (the build design, line 429). `ur-queue` passes. `ur-queue-long` (13 characters), `Ur-queue`, `1round`, and `ur_queue` do not.

Out of scope: the round report as an item and the `report` command (pass Q4), a `--round` option on `digest`, the round on the history page's rows, the round in the `packets.jsonl` log line, the order the app opens the next item in after a decision, the `approval-packet` skill's list of fact-file keys (pass W4, in another repo), and backlog items #38, #41, #49, and #64. The live server is never rebuilt or restarted in this pass. The command changes reach the live queue when the main checkout pulls, because `~/.local/bin/approval-queue` points there (mechanism 21). The page changes reach it when the conductor runs `approval-queue restart` after the PR lands.
