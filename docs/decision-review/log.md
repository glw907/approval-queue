# Decision review log

One line per host per month, quiet months included, so a lost log costs at most one month of history. The monthly review session (`prompt.txt`) appends the line; the counts come from that host's `decisions.jsonl` and `refusals.jsonl`.

Line shape:

```text
<YYYY-MM> <host>: <packets> packets, <asks> asks, <routine> routine parts, <overrides> overrides, <refusals> refusals, <proposals> proposals sent
```
