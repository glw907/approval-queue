# Sample queue redesign

You are asked to approve a design that changes the queue page. If you approve, a session plans the work under the design and builds it in one pass.

The page becomes one reading column with a notes margin. The design lists what it removes and what it keeps, and it states how each behavior fails.

You have one call to make: whether the redesign drops the contents rail.

## Deep dive

Why the page is one column, and what the notes margin replaces.

### Why one column

The history shows where you act, and the page spreads the decision across three columns. One column lets you decide from the opening and the calls.

## Your calls

The one call decides how much of the old page stays.

### Should the redesign drop the contents rail?

Context: The rail lists the page's sections beside the text. The notes margin sits in the same place on the right.

Risk: A reader who used the rail to jump between sections loses that route.

Recommendation: Drop it. The history shows no use of the rail in 364 decided items.
