# Water a houseplant

:::reader
**Who reads it.** Someone who keeps one plant on a windowsill.
:::

## Check the soil

*Shows the reader how to tell when the plant needs water.*

- Press a finger into the soil up to the first knuckle. :fact[ab12]
- Dry soil at that depth means the plant needs water. :fact[cd34]

## Fix a drooping plant

*Gets the reader from a drooping plant to its cause.*

:::row
**Symptom.** The leaves droop although the soil is wet.
**Cause.** The pot has no drainage hole, so the roots sit in water. :fact[ef56]
**Fix.** Move the plant to a pot with a drainage hole.
:::

- Water again only once the soil has dried.
