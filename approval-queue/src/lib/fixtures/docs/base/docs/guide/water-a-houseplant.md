# Water a houseplant

Water a houseplant when the top inch of soil is dry.

## Check the soil

Press a finger into the soil up to the first knuckle. Dry soil at that depth means the plant needs water.

## Water the plant

Pour slowly until water runs out of the drainage holes. Empty the saucer after ten minutes.

## Fix a drooping plant

A drooping plant is more often overwatered than thirsty. Check the soil before you pour.
